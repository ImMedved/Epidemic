## Responsibility and ownership

Owns demand/request identity, request progress, chunk state, bounded progressive streaming and retryable residency/commit cleanup; external world, persistence, resource and residency authority stays behind ports.

## Authoritative state and indexes

Authoritative state is requests, demands, chunk-to-current-request mapping, observable chunk states, revisions/completion sequence, statistics and shutdown state. Accepted step result and cleanup-prefix flags are private reconciliation state.

## Public and external contract

Runtime-owned allocation/publication failure occurs before mutation or preserves a durable accepted result/cleanup owner. Successful external ExecuteStep/cleanup prefixes are not repeated after a later local failure.

## Lifecycle and cleanup

Activation/deactivation/unload and Runtime Shutdown are explicit; once shutdown begins new demand is rejected and repeated Shutdown continues unfinished cleanup without repeating completed prefixes.

## Limits, counters and identity

Item, CPU-time and byte budgets are bounded and checked; request/demand IDs, generations, request revisions and completion sequence are non-wrapping.

## Persistence applicability

No module-local snapshot/restore API exists. Persistent world/resource data stays behind dependency ports; retry state is transient Runtime ownership.
