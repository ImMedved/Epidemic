# Time

Status: B01 Goal 4 review. Local module contract only.

Responsibility: gameplay clocks, calendar definitions, scheduled actions, recurrence/catch-up, cancellation, synchronization, due-trigger collection, scheduler revision/journal continuity and snapshot/restore. Public contract: `gameplay_time.h`.

Authoritative state: registered clocks/actions, clock state, schedules, due index, schedule ID generator, scheduler revision and externally synchronized-clock metadata. Derived state is reconstructed from validated authoritative records.

Dependencies: Framework Foundation only. The module does not own Runtime time; cross-layer synchronization is performed through integration code.

Lifecycle and identity: schedule IDs are monotonic and non-reused. Create/reschedule/cancel and terminal schedule semantics are explicit. Same-timestamp trigger order is deterministic. Budget-limited collection defers remaining work without losing schedules.

Persistence boundary: snapshot/restore covers clocks, schedules, generators, revisions and synchronization metadata. Restore validates duplicate IDs, references and generator continuity before commit. Whole-engine ordered restore is Goal 5.

Failure and atomicity: registration, scheduling, rescheduling, due collection and synchronization must not expose partial record/index/revision state when local publication fails.

Limits and threading: fixed-width gameplay time and ID/revision spaces are checked for overflow/exhaustion. The service is synchronous and caller-serialized; no internal worker ownership.

Local invariants and tests: `time_tests.cpp` covers clock registration, recurrence, catch-up/large jumps, cancellation, deterministic due ordering, budget deferral, snapshot roundtrip/invalid restore, generator/revision continuity and mutation atomicity.
